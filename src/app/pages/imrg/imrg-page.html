IMRG Research Grant

Interdisciplinary Multidisciplinary Research Grant

The IMRG grant scheme encourages cross-departmental research collaborations within KSRCT. It aims to foster interdisciplinary research projects bringing together expertise from Engineering, Basic Sciences, Biotechnology, and Information Technology.
